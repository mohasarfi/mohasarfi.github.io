05 / Projects

On the bench right now

Professional research at AsterQuanta and independent projects on my own time.

ML-based Analog Circuit Design

AT WORK
actively building at AsterQuanta

Leading research on RL and generative models for automated analog circuit design at AsterQuanta: from topology generation to multi-objective optimization.

The core challenge is that generating a circuit topology and optimizing its parameters can’t be treated as two separate steps; they’re deeply coupled. The research tackles this as a unified problem using a bi-level framework, where a generative model proposes circuit structures and an RL agent handles device-level sizing across multiple competing objectives.

Teach WALL·E to find the plant

A tiny Q-learning agent you can watch learn. Drop trash cubes to make it harder.

WALL·E starts knowing nothing. Each step costs a little, reaching the plant pays off, and it updates a value for every square with Q(s,a) ← Q + α[r + γ·max Q(s′,·) − Q]. Brighter squares are ones it now believes lead to the plant. Click squares to add or clear trash.
